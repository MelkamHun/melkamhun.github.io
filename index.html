Melkamu Hunegnaw Asmare

Advancing AI and technology for
affordable, accessible healthcare.

I am a postdoctoral researcher and research coordinator at the Leuven Centre for Affordable Health Technology and eMedia Research Lab, KU Leuven. My work connects health AI, biomedical signal processing and digital health with the practical needs of healthcare systems.

I develop and co-lead international projects in digital care, responsible AI and inclusive education. Across research and teaching, I focus on technologies that are clinically useful, affordable and implementable in settings with limited resources.

I received my PhD in Engineering Technology from KU Leuven in 2022, supervised by Prof. Bart Vanrumste, with research on rheumatic heart disease detection. My academic and professional experience spans Africa, Asia and Europe.

Research interests

01

AI for screening & diagnosis

Machine learning for ECG, heart sounds and medical imaging, with applications in rheumatic heart disease and glaucoma screening. A focus on robust evaluation and accessible diagnostic tools.

02

Affordable & sustainable health technology

Healthcare technologies designed around affordability, usability, maintenance and real clinical workflows. Human-centred design and implementation in resource-constrained settings.

03

Equitable digital health & responsible AI

Explainable AI, health data governance and inclusive digital care. International partnerships that connect technical research with local needs, education and health-system capacity.

Research projects

I contribute as a proposal developer and co-PI / project co-promoter to collaborations across digital health, education and responsible technology.

Horizon Europe · MSCA Doctoral NetworkJustHealthEthics and governance for just and trustworthy healthcare AI

Developing a decolonized ethics and governance framework for healthcare AI through doctoral training, technical research and clinical applications. My research contributions connect to rheumatic heart disease screening and staging.

Project website ↗
VLIR-UOS TEAM · 2026–2031DiaLink PalestineDigital support for continuity of diabetes care

A partnership with Palestine Polytechnic University to develop mobile support for Type 2 diabetes care, explainable clinical decision support and a Digital Health Living Lab. The project connects clinical evaluation, digital health education and service adoption in conflict-affected settings.

VLIR-UOS TEAM · 2026–2031TRUSTTrauma-informed, Resilient, User-led Support Toolkit

Collaboration with Mekelle and Aksum universities on digital mental health support for war-affected youth in Tigray. The project brings together participatory design, clinical evaluation, safeguarding and data governance.

VLIR-UOS TEAM · 2026–2031EQUIPInclusive learning for frontline health and agriculture workers

With Arba Minch University and Arba Minch College of Teacher Education, EQUIP aims to develop accessible micro-certifications, evaluate learning in practice and embed training in continuing professional development.

VLIR-UOS Short Initiative · 2025–2027AISLEAI-Supported Inclusive Learning Environment

A partnership with Mekelle University on AI-assisted and tactile learning tools for visually impaired students in Tigray. The programme combines educator training, accessible curriculum development and evaluation with learners and community partners.

KU Leuven · Global Minds Open Faculty ProjectsGlobal Affordable Health Technology WeekInternational collaboration for accessible healthcare

A university–industry initiative at Group T that connects partners around affordable healthcare, curriculum development and future research collaborations, including collaboration with Ugani Prosthetics.
